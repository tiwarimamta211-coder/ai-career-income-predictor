    📘 Is Tool Ko Kaise Use Karein (Hindi Guide)

    Yeh AI Career Income Predictor aapko batata hai ki agle 1, 3, 5 aur 10 saal mein aapki income kitni ho sakti hai — aapke role, experience, skills, city aur upskilling ke basis par.

    1. Sabse pehle apna current role, industry, experience aur annual income daalein.
    2. Apni education, city tier aur upskilling commitment select karein.
    3. Apne key skills chips par click karke select karein (jitne applicable hain).
    4. "⚡ Predict My Income Growth" button dabayein — turant aapko income forecast chart, AI disruption risk, career path suggestions aur skill gap recommendations milenge.
    5. Agar aapke paas Anthropic API key hai, toh "🤖 Get Personalized AI Career Roadmap" section mein daal kar ek detailed, personalized roadmap generate kar sakte hain. Key nahi hai toh chinta mat karo — tool khud ek built-in rule-based roadmap bana dega, bilkul free.
    6. Har prediction automatically "Your Past Predictions" section mein save ho jaata hai (aapke browser mein hi, kahin aur nahi jaata) — future mein compare karne ke liye.

    Tip: Yeh tool sirf ek estimate deta hai, based on industry averages aur trends — real income growth aapke performance, negotiation aur market conditions par bhi depend karta hai. Use it as a directional guide, financial advice nahi hai yeh.
